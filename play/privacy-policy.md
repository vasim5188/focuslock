# Focus Lock — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

Focus Lock is a focus and app-blocking app for Android, published by Vasim Akram
Shaik. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

Focus Lock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no
accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers. Everything the app
stores stays on your device.

## Information we collect

**None.** Focus Lock does not collect personal information. We cannot see which
apps you block, when you use the app, or anything else about your device.

The app has no user accounts and never asks you to sign in.

## Information stored on your device

Focus Lock stores the following locally, so that it can work:

- The apps you have chosen to block, and the time window set for each.
- Your unlock history: pending waits, temporary unlock grants, and a count of
  unlocks per day, used to apply escalating wait times.
- App preferences: your theme choice, whether onboarding is complete, and
  whether the optional app lock is enabled.

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to the cloud — Android
backup is explicitly disabled for this app. Uninstalling Focus Lock permanently
deletes all of it.

## Permissions, and why each is needed

**Accessibility Service.** Focus Lock uses Android's Accessibility Service to
detect which app is currently in the foreground. This is the only way an app
can tell that you have opened a blocked app so that it can show the blocking
screen at the right moment.

The service reads only the package name of the app in the foreground. It does
not read the content of your screen, does not record what you type, does not
capture passwords or messages, and does not transmit anything anywhere. No
information obtained through the Accessibility Service is collected, stored
beyond what is described above, or shared with anyone.

You grant this permission explicitly in Android Settings, and you can revoke it
at any time from Settings > Accessibility, or from within Focus Lock under
Permissions.

**Display over other apps.** Used to draw the blocking screen on top of a
blocked app. It is shown only while one of your own protected apps is open
during a window you configured.

**Notifications and foreground service.** Focus Lock runs a foreground service
with an ongoing notification so that Android keeps protection running in the
background. Without it, the system may stop the app and blocking would fail.

**Run at startup.** Lets protection resume after you restart your phone.

**Biometric / device credential.** If you turn on the optional app lock, Focus
Lock asks Android to verify you using your existing fingerprint, PIN, pattern,
or password. Verification is performed entirely by Android. Focus Lock never
sees, receives, or stores your biometric data or your PIN — it is told only
whether authentication succeeded.

## Information we share

Nothing. We do not sell, rent, or share data, because we do not collect any.
Focus Lock makes no network requests.

## Children

Focus Lock is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any
information from anyone, including children.
