## Full description  (max 4000)

```
Focus Lock helps you stay off the apps that pull you away from what you meant to do.

Pick the apps you want protecting yourself from, set the hours you want them blocked, and Focus Lock steps in when you reach for them.

WHAT IT DOES

• Choose up to 2 apps to block
• Set one weekly time window per app, including windows that cross midnight
• Open a blocked app during its window and a block screen appears
• Go back, or wait it out to earn a 10-minute unlock
• Works completely offline

ESCALATING WAITS

The first unlock asks for one minute. The next asks for three, then seven, then fifteen, then thirty or more. The friction grows with the habit, so acting on impulse costs more than acting with intent. Cancel a wait before it finishes and it doesn't count against you.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account. No sign-in. No analytics. No ads. No servers. Focus Lock makes no network requests at all. Everything it stores — your app list, your schedules and your unlock history — stays on your phone, and uninstalling deletes all of it.

OPTIONAL APP LOCK

Lock Focus Lock itself behind your phone's existing fingerprint, PIN or pattern, so changing your own rules takes a deliberate step rather than a reflex.

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE DISCLOSURE

Focus Lock uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect which app is currently in the foreground. This is what lets it show the block screen at the moment you open a protected app.

The service reads only the package name of the foreground app. It does not read the content of your screen, does not record what you type, and does not capture passwords or messages. Nothing it observes is collected, transmitted or shared with anyone. You grant this permission yourself, and you can revoke it at any time in Android Settings.

WHAT YOU WILL BE ASKED TO GRANT

• Accessibility — to detect which app is in the foreground
• Display over other apps — to show the block screen
• Notifications — to keep protection running in the background

Focus Lock walks you through each one, and explains what it is for before asking.
```
